A photo editor with layers for Android tablets and Linux

photographics opens PSD, TIFF and GIMP files with all their layers and saves them the same way. You retouch with the pen on the tablet or with mouse and graphics tablet on a Linux desktop, and colour profiles are kept. Free, open source and no account needed.

Layers and masks

Tools on the left, layers on the right, the image in the middle. Adjustments sit on their own layers above the photo and stay editable until the end; masks limit them to parts of the image.

  • Pixel, text and smart object layers, groups
  • 12 adjustment layers, including per-channel curves with histogram, levels, colour balance and black & white
  • All 27 Photoshop blend modes
  • Layer masks and clipping masks
  • Photoshop layer styles: shadows, glows, stroke, bevel, satin and overlays, rendered and editable
  • Shape layers, pen tool, paths and alpha channels
  • Transform with skew, distort and perspective, lossless as a smart object

Retouching with the pen

Pen pressure controls size, opacity and flow. Your hand can rest on the display without painting. Two fingers zoom, and a short two-finger tap undoes the last step.

  • Spot healing, healing brush, patch, clone stamp and content-aware fill
  • Dodge, burn, sponge, smudge and liquify
  • Brushes with custom tips, scattering and smoothing, pencil
  • Paint bucket, fill command, background and magic eraser
  • Retouch on an empty layer and leave the original untouched

Colour management and looks

Every document keeps its colour profile. Before printing, the soft proof shows how the image will look with your print shop's profile and marks colours that are out of gamut.

  • sRGB, Display P3, Adobe RGB, ProPhoto and your own ICC profiles
  • Assign or convert profiles with all four rendering intents
  • Colour lookup tables in .cube and .3dl format, export your own adjustments as .cube
  • 15 film looks, from slide film to black-and-white infrared

New: continue on a Linux desktop

Since version 0.4, photographics is also available for Linux, as a GNOME app with menus, tool options and a layers panel that work like Photoshop. It shares its core with the tablet version, so files, tools and results are identical.

  • Ships as a Flatpak for any distribution, Java included
  • Photoshop shortcuts; brush size and hardness with Alt + right mouse button
  • Select several layers, reorder them by dragging, group and merge
  • Filter gallery with 47 effects, layer style dialog, printing and soft proofing

The same file, on the road and at the desk

A PSD from the studio opens on the tablet with its layers, masks, groups and adjustment layers. Whatever photographics cannot display yet, pattern overlays for example, stays in the file and is written back unchanged when you save.

Open
PSD and PSB, TIFF, GIMP (XCF), PNG, JPEG, WebP and HEIF
Save
PSD, TIFF and XCF with all layers
Export
PNG and JPEG, with colour profile or converted to sRGB
Bit depth
Reads 8, 16 and 32 bit, edits with 8 or 16 bit per channel

Also included

On-device AI

Remove objects and upscale images up to 4×. The models are downloaded only when you ask for them and run offline.

Cropping

Aspect ratios, target size in cm or inches, straighten and perspective crop.

Guides

Rulers, guide layouts and smart guides, kept in PSD and XCF files.

Swatches

Load and save swatches from Photoshop, Illustrator and GIMP (.aco, .ase, .gpl).

Keyboard and mouse

With a keyboard cover, a Chromebook or Samsung DeX, the familiar Photoshop shortcuts work.

Large files

Image data lives outside the app memory limit, filters run on all cores, and moving and transforming run on the GPU.

Selections

Magic wand, quick selection, object selection, lasso and polygonal lasso. Selections can be lifted, moved and freely transformed.

Gradients

Linear, radial and reflected, on layers or straight into masks, for example as a graduated filter for the sky.

Filter gallery

47 artistic effects in six groups, combinable as a stack, with a preview of the whole image.

English and German

The interface follows the system language and can be switched in the settings.

Roadmap

What is planned next. The order may change and there are no fixed dates. Suggestions are welcome. New in 0.4: the Linux version, editable layer styles, shapes, pen tool and paths, filter gallery, 16-bit editing, paint bucket and more erasers, quick selection, and copy and paste.

Next

  • Autosave and recovery after crashes
  • Custom fonts (TTF, OTF)
  • History with snapshots
  • Select subject and sky with on-device AI, refine edges for hair

After that

  • Frequency separation as a retouching helper
  • Batch export in several sizes and formats
  • Patterns and pattern overlay

Later

  • CMYK documents and CMYK export
  • Display colour management
  • Full compositing on the GPU
  • Version for iPad

Download photographics

Both versions are available on GitHub. On Android, the arm64 APK is the right one for almost all current tablets and phones. For Linux there is a Flatpak repository of its own: “Flatpak for Linux” opens the installation in GNOME Software or Discover, and updates arrive automatically. In a terminal: flatpak install --user https://photographics.andreasbartelmess.de/flatpak/photographics.flatpakref

APK for Android Flatpak for Linux Source code

Android 8.1 or newer, a tablet with a pen is recommended. Linux with Flatpak, x86_64. The interface is available in English and German.

An early preview

photographics is at an early stage. A lot already works, some things are missing or will still change. Please keep a backup of important files.

Feedback and bug reports help a lot. Anyone who wants to help develop, translate or support the project in another way is very welcome.
